# The Pirate King

Leader of the Pirates of Penzance, and by his own account rather proud of the job — "it is, it is a glorious thing to be a Pirate King." Enforces the crew's one hard rule without exception: never harm an orphan, a rule the entire supporting cast quickly learns to exploit by claiming orphanhood on the spot.

Later revealed, along with the rest of the crew, to be a nobleman who has gone wrong rather than a career criminal — which, under the operetta's own logic, is grounds for immediate forgiveness.
