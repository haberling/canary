# Frederic

A pirate apprentice by accident of mishearing — his nursemaid Ruth was told to apprentice him to a *pilot* and misheard *pirate*. Having just turned twenty-one, he believes himself free of his indenture and resolves to help destroy the crew he grew up with, purely out of an overdeveloped sense of duty rather than any real grudge.

Falls in love with Mabel almost the instant he sees her. Later discovered to have been born on February 29th, which — by the strict wording of his indenture — means he won't reach his majority again until 1940.

> A paradox, a paradox, a most ingenious paradox!<br>
> We've quips and quibbles heard in flocks, but none to beat this paradox!
