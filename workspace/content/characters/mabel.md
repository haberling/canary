# Mabel

The youngest of Major-General Stanley's many daughters. Falls for Frederic as quickly as he falls for her, and stands by him without hesitation once the business of his indenture and its arithmetic complications comes to light.

Best known for the soprano showpiece "Poor Wandering One," which she sings to reassure Frederic (and, not incidentally, to show off some genuinely difficult coloratura) shortly after they meet.
