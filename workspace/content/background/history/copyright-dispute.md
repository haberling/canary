# The Copyright Dispute

*H.M.S. Pinafore* had been a runaway hit in New York — performed by unlicensed American companies who paid Gilbert and Sullivan nothing at all, since American copyright law at the time gave a British work no protection. For *Pirates*, they closed the loophole directly: stage the world premiere in New York itself, with an authorized company, a day before the London opening. An American copyright required first publication in the US, so this reversed the usual order on purpose.

It mostly worked. It didn't stop every unlicensed production that followed, but it was a deliberate, structural response to a real problem, not an afterthought.
